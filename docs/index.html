01 / The idea

Give road teams a clearer place to start.

Road inspections can be slow, manual, and reactive. AURA explores how computer vision can surface visible pavement distress from everyday road footage, helping cities understand where closer inspection may be needed.

Core capabilities

One pipeline.
Three useful views.

From the first upload to the final summary, AURA keeps the workflow focused on clear, reviewable information.

01

Detect damage

A custom-trained YOLOv8s model marks visible cracks and potholes in uploaded photos and recorded clips.

02

Map the context

When supported GPS metadata is available, AURA places the analyzed media on a map for useful location context.

03

Track through video

Persistent BoT-SORT IDs help reduce repeated counts as the same road hazard appears across consecutive frames.

Built to recognize

Four forms of pavement distress.

D00 Longitudinal cracks
D10 Transverse cracks
D20 Alligator cracking
D40 Potholes
